Meet Spoiled.

Spoiled is a Jeddah catering business serving frozen yogurt, matcha and coffee. Our Signature Cart and Premium Cart bring the service to private celebrations, corporate events and campuses.

How Booking Works

Booking your Spoiled catering experience is simple!

  1. Explore Our Packages: Browse our catering packages and choose the one that best suits your event.
  2. Customize Your Event: Select your preferred services and number of guests.
  3. Submit Your Request: Fill out the booking form with your event details.
  4. Wait for Confirmation: Our team will review your request and contact you to confirm the booking.
